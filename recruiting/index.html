02 / Plan

Know what each stage asks of you

Processes vary by company, role, and level. Use only the stages in your actual schedule.

  1. Application & resume

    Tailor your evidence to the role. Be ready to explain each project, result, and technology.

  2. Recruiter screen

    Prepare a short introduction, why this role, logistics, and questions about the exact process.

  3. Online assessment

    Practice the platform, read its rules, secure a correct core solution, and test edge cases.

  4. Live coding & pairing

    Clarify the problem, explain your approach, write runnable code, test it, and respond to feedback.

  5. Project deep dive

    Know your ownership, architecture, tradeoffs, results, and what you would change.

  6. Take-home assignment

    Confirm scope and timebox; build the required path first, test it, and document decisions.

  7. System design

    Clarify requirements and scale, sketch the main flow, then discuss bottlenecks and tradeoffs.

  8. Behavioral interview

    Use honest STAR-R stories that show your actions, outcomes, and reflection.

  9. Final loop & follow-up

    Treat each round as fresh, bring questions, and follow up after the promised timeline.

04 / Before the call

Final preparation checklist

  • Confirm stages, duration, tools, languages, and permitted resources.
  • Practice a concise introduction and a specific reason for this role.
  • Solve coding problems aloud, test your code, and state complexity.
  • Explain two projects: problem, ownership, design, tradeoffs, impact, and reflection.
  • Prepare to defend take-home choices and use a design framework if required.
  • Prepare six to eight honest STAR-R stories tied to the role’s values.
  • Bring questions for the recruiter, manager, and engineers.
  • Check your connection, camera, microphone, editor, time zone, and meeting links.

Interview processes and tool policies change. Your latest recruiter instructions and role-specific materials take precedence. Resource links were collected in September 2026.
